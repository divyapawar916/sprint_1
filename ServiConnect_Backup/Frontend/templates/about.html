What is ServiConnect?

ServiConnect is a local services management platform designed to connect customers with service providers in their area.

Customers can explore available services, view service providers, make bookings, manage their bookings and share reviews.

Service providers can manage their services, receive customer requests, update booking status and track their earnings.
